On Blogging

So I think I've got this in pipeline for quite a while. Blogging.

When I was younger I used to draw a lot, some days the only thing I did was draw and I miss getting into the flow zone. The flow zone is when time disappears, you're just you and what you're doing. The outside world is gone. And that the outside world disappears is happening less and less. Even though I've disabled 95% of all notifications on my phone, it still nudges me. Breaktime it says, time to get ready for that, time to discusss kids' practice. Time to get skis ready, slopes are icy, edges needs sharpening. It's part of being a parent, you're responsible the small peoples' well being. But it takes out your flow zone time.

Enter blogging.

I'm fairly quick at the keyboard, even though I haven't done speed practice for a long time... It would be weird otherwise, seeing as I'm a coder in my normal job. But anyway, back to creative output.

I've seen more and more posts about doing something creative and I remember the old days, back in the 90's and perhaps early 2000's, when Internet was weird and fun. I want to get back to that feeling again. It's not so polished, and overworked, and boring.

I started a Jekyll blog over on github, and even though it was markdown I felt like I was too far away from the nuts and bolts. So I took another look at Neocities (look at that, I just created a <a> element for the Neocities link :D ), together with the Zonelets framework and it's just the perfect amount of help I need to get going but still have access to the nuts and bolts I need. Perfect!

With Zonelets you download a webpage, and then just delete the stuff you don't want and add the stuff you want. You have a index.html and directory posts where you place your blog posts. You edit the script.js in the bottom and add you post to a postArrray.

When you're done, you drag and drop the pages into the Neocities admin page, done and done.
